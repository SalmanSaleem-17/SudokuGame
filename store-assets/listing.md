## App name (30 max)

## Short description (80 max)

Classic 9x9 Sudoku. Play offline with step-by-step hints and a daily puzzle.

## Full description (4000 max)

Sudoku Studio is a clean, classic 9x9 Sudoku game that works completely offline. Open it, pick a difficulty and play. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

PUZZLES FOR EVERY LEVEL
• Four difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert
• 140 verified puzzles, each with exactly one solution
• Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, with no guessing

A NEW PUZZLE EVERY DAY
• One Daily Puzzle for each calendar date
• A calendar that shows the days you have completed
• A puzzle you start before midnight still counts for the day you started it

HINTS THAT TEACH
• Step-by-step hints explain the reasoning before placing anything
• Hints highlight the cells involved, so you can see why the move works
• Every puzzle includes one free hint

PRACTICE THE TECHNIQUES
• Free drills for naked singles, hidden singles and locked candidates
• Each drill uses a real board position and explains the answer
• After a puzzle, see which technique it used and practise it

COMFORTABLE TO PLAY
• Pencil notes, with optional smart notes that tidy themselves as you fill cells
• Undo, erase and number-first entry
• Optional timer and mistake feedback
• Highlights for matching digits, rows, columns and boxes
• Your game is saved automatically, so you can stop and resume any time
• Haptics and reduced-motion options

FAIR AND PRIVATE
• No banner ads and no ads between puzzles
• The only ad is an optional video you can choose to watch for extra hint credits
• No puzzle, difficulty or daily challenge is locked behind an ad
• Your progress and settings stay on your device

Sudoku Studio is free. Hint credits are stored only on your device and are lost if you uninstall the app.
